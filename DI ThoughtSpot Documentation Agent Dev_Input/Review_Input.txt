Reviewer Validation Checklist

Completeness Checks

- Verify that all mandatory sections specified in the agent instructions are present in the output.
- Confirm that all required fields, columns, or analyses mentioned in the instructions are included.
- Check that all referenced entities, files, or components from the instructions are addressed.
- Ensure that all required validations and use cases from the instructions are covered.
- Validate that all mandatory headers, classifications, tags, or statuses required by the instructions are included.
- Confirm that all formatting requirements from the instructions are followed.
- Verify that no requested section from the instructions is skipped.
- Ensure that all gap or missing item handling requirements from the instructions are addressed.

Accuracy Checks

- Confirm that the output aligns with the provided instructions.
- Ensure that no hallucinated content or unsupported assumptions are present.
- Check that all dependency and technical mappings are logically correct as per the instructions.
- Validate that all business rule interpretations are correct and consistent with the instructions.
- Ensure that terminology and formatting standards from the instructions are used consistently.
- Confirm that no false-positive gaps or false-negative omissions occur based on the instructions.
- Verify that all relationships between entities or components are correctly interpreted as per the instructions.
- Ensure that no instruction is misinterpreted or only partially implemented.

Efficiency Checks

- Verify that duplicate analysis and redundant explanations are avoided.
- Ensure that repeated gap entries and duplicate dependency mappings are eliminated.
- Confirm that the output remains concise, structured, and free of unnecessary sections.
- Check that excessive verbosity is avoided and formatting remains clean and optimized.
- Ensure that overlapping findings are consolidated and similar findings are logically grouped where appropriate.
- Validate that output structure improves readability and review efficiency.
- Confirm that large sections are summarized efficiently where allowed by the instructions.
- Ensure that redundant classifications, tags, and validations are avoided.
